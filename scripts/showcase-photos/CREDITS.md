# Showcase dish photos

Photos for the Kaaram Kitchen showcase restaurant come from Unsplash (https://unsplash.com)
under the Unsplash License, which allows free commercial use. Each file is named after the
dish; its Unsplash photo id is in ../showcase-photos.json (https://unsplash.com/photos/<id>).
Some show a close match rather than the exact dish. Real restaurants upload their own photos
from the admin dashboard.
